About AgriSense

A TinyML soil-intelligence platform for classrooms and small fields. AgriSense pairs a low-cost RS485 soil probe (built around an ESP32-S3) with a lightweight k-nearest-neighbors model trained on a 2,200-row reference dataset of 22 Philippine crops (rice, corn, banana, coconut, cassava, calamansi, abaca and more).

{{ icon('radio-tower',22) }}
Seven Raw Readings
Nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, pH, temperature, and humidity — captured by a field probe or entered by hand.
{{ icon('cpu',22) }}
Lightweight ML
A 5-neighbor KNN classifier turns those readings into a soil health score and a ranked list of suitable crops.
{{ icon('graduation',22) }}
Built for Learning
Every number is paired with a plain-language explanation a student can actually use.
How a Reading Becomes a Recommendation
Health scoreEvery reading is scored against agronomic optimal ranges to produce a 0–100 health score and a letter grade.
Crop comfort bandsEach of the 22 crops has a learned band for every feature (the 10th–90th percentile of readings that produced that label).
Match percentageA reading's match against a crop is the share of features that fall inside its band. The single best match also comes from a 5-neighbor KNN classifier.
Hardware
ESP32-S3 + RS485 probeA low-cost soil probe that can run unattended in the field.
Straight to the dashboardField devices POST JSON to /api/esp32, so probe readings appear next to manual entries.
One shared historyDevice and manual readings share the same history, charts, and crop picks.
