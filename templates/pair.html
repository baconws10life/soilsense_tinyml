Your soil scanner

The website looks for your AgriSense scanner for you — no codes to type. Connect to a free scanner, press Scan now here or on the scanner itself, and your reading appears here.

How it works

  1. Power on the scanner. Its screen is one big SCAN NOW button.
  2. Press Find device. Scanners that are online show up automatically.
  3. Press Connect. If someone else is already using it, it is locked until they disconnect.
  4. Press Scan now on this page (or touch the scanner’s screen). Keep the probe in the soil until your reading pops up. Disconnect when you are done so the next person can use it.

Stuck behind someone who forgot to disconnect? Ask an admin — they can disconnect any scanner.
