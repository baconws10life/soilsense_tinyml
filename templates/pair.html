How it works

  1. Power on the scanner. Its screen is one big SCAN NOW button.
  2. Press Find device. Scanners that are online show up automatically.
  3. Press Connect. If someone else is already using it, it is locked until they disconnect.
  4. Press the scanner’s screen to scan. Disconnect when you are done so the next person can use it.

Stuck behind someone who forgot to disconnect? Ask an admin — they can disconnect any scanner.
